The creatures I draw learn to walk by themselves

My longest-running project is a series of evolution games. The idea is simple: you design the creature — its body is yours — and then the game takes over. A neural-network brain and a genetic algorithm evolve how the body moves, generation after generation, in a deterministic physics simulation.

The fun part is that evolution rarely does what you expect. Sometimes a body I designed to walk ends up rolling, hopping, or dragging itself in a way that is completely logical once you see it — and sometimes it moves exactly the way I imagined, which feels even better.

There is also an online side: players can compare and battle creatures other people designed. Seeing someone else's strange, brilliant body plan is half the motivation to keep building.

Current status: the latest version is the 8th in the series, and I'm now exploring a new one where the creature's body starts as pixel art — you draw it dot by dot, and the drawing becomes the body that evolves.
